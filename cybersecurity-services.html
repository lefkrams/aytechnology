Frequently Asked Questions About Cybersecurity

Why does a small business need cybersecurity?

Small businesses rely on email, online accounts, cloud platforms and connected devices. Cybersecurity helps reduce the risk of unauthorised access, phishing, malware, data loss and business disruption.

Can you review our current cybersecurity setup?

Yes. We can review suitable parts of your current setup and recommend practical improvements for accounts, devices, email, networks, cloud services and data protection.

Do you help with phishing and email security?

Yes. We can help improve email security settings, strengthen account access and provide practical guidance for recognising suspicious links, attachments and impersonation attempts.

Do you provide cybersecurity services in Perth?

Yes. AY Technology supports businesses across Perth and Western Australia. Many security reviews and configuration tasks can be completed remotely, with on-site assistance available for suitable work.

Can cybersecurity remove every business risk?

No security measure can remove every risk. A layered approach using secure account settings, protected devices, backups, staff awareness and ongoing maintenance can significantly improve your security posture.
