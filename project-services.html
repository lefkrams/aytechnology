Frequently asked questions

Questions about Project Services

Can you support a one-off drafting project?

You can submit the requirements for a one-off job. We will review the scope and confirm whether suitable resources and skills are available before providing a quotation.

Can I request a dedicated CAD drafter?

Yes, you can enquire about ongoing drafting personnel. Suitable experience, supervision, availability, work arrangements and pricing would need to be discussed first.

Which CAD software and file formats do you support?

Please provide your required software, file types and version requirements when enquiring. We will confirm compatibility before accepting the work.

Do you provide engineering certification or architectural approval?

Drafting and documentation support does not automatically include professional design certification, building approvals or regulated architectural or engineering services. Where required, these must be carried out or signed off by appropriately qualified and registered professionals.

Can I send confidential drawings?

Let us know if your project requires a confidentiality agreement or a specified secure file-sharing method. Please do not send sensitive project files until an appropriate transfer method has been agreed.

Have a drafting project in mind?

Tell us whether you need a defined drawing package or ongoing CAD drafting support. We'll review your requirements and discuss the next step.
