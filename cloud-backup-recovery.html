Frequently Asked Questions About Cloud Backup

Why does a business need cloud backup?

Cloud backup helps protect important business information from accidental deletion, device failure, cyber incidents and other disruptions. It also provides a separate copy that may be used when recovery is required.

Can you back up Microsoft 365 data?

Suitable Microsoft 365 data, including email and files, can be protected using an appropriate third-party backup solution. The exact coverage depends on the selected service and configuration.

How often should business data be backed up?

The right backup frequency depends on how often your data changes and how much information your business can afford to recreate. Many businesses use automated daily backups, while critical systems may require more frequent protection.

Do you test whether backups can be restored?

Backup monitoring and suitable recovery testing can be included or arranged depending on the selected solution. Testing helps confirm that protected information can be accessed when needed.

Do you provide cloud backup services in Perth?

Yes. AY Technology supports businesses across Perth and Western Australia. Many backup configuration, monitoring and recovery tasks can be completed remotely.
