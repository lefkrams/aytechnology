Is the IT assessment really free?

Yes. The initial review of the information you submit is free and carries no obligation. More detailed technical audits, on-site work or implementation may require a separate quote.

Do you need access to our systems?

Not for the initial enquiry. AY Technology begins with the information you provide. Any remote or on-site access requirements will be explained before a deeper technical assessment.

What businesses is this suitable for?

The assessment is intended for small and medium-sized businesses that are experiencing recurring IT issues, reviewing their current setup or planning technology improvements.

How quickly will you contact me?

AY Technology aims to review new enquiries within 24 hours. For urgent assistance, call 0423 384 874.
