Frequently Asked Questions

What can remote network monitoring track?

Depending on the agreed setup, monitoring may track selected network devices, servers, computers, connectivity, availability and system-health indicators.

Does monitoring fix every problem automatically?

No. Monitoring mainly provides visibility and alerts. Some supported actions may be automated, while other issues require investigation or approval.

Is network monitoring suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Monitoring can be scaled around the number of devices, locations and systems a small or medium-sized business needs to watch.

Can you monitor multiple business locations?

Monitoring may be configured across multiple suitable locations, subject to internet access, equipment compatibility and the agreed service scope.

Does monitoring replace cybersecurity?

No. Monitoring supports visibility, but businesses still need appropriate cybersecurity controls, backups, access management and user practices.
