Frequently Asked Questions About Virtual Assistants

What tasks can a virtual assistant help with?

A virtual assistant can support routine administrative and operational tasks such as email organisation, scheduling, data entry, research, document preparation, customer follow-ups and CRM updates. The exact task list is agreed according to your business needs.

Can I use a virtual assistant part-time?

Yes. Virtual assistant support can be arranged around an agreed workload, schedule and scope. This can suit businesses that need ongoing part-time assistance or help with a defined group of tasks.

How do you choose a suitable virtual assistant?

We first discuss your tasks, tools, required working hours and communication preferences. This information is then used to identify suitable support and define clear responsibilities before work begins.

Can a virtual assistant support customers?

A virtual assistant may assist with routine customer enquiries, follow-ups, appointment confirmations and administrative communication using approved scripts, procedures and escalation rules.

Do you provide virtual assistant services across Australia?

Yes. AY Technology provides remote virtual assistant support for businesses across Australia, including Perth and Western Australia. Availability and working arrangements are discussed before the service begins.
