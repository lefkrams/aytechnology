Frequently Asked Questions

What is cloud telephony?

Cloud telephony delivers business phone functions through internet-based services rather than relying only on a traditional on-site phone system.

Can staff use the system while working remotely?

Yes. Depending on the selected platform and configuration, staff may use supported desk phones, computers or mobile applications.

Can we keep our existing business number?

Number-porting options depend on the number, provider and service availability. AY Technology can review your requirements and discuss suitable options.

Can you help plan call routing?

Yes. We can discuss departments, opening hours, overflow handling, voicemail and other call-flow requirements.

Do you provide support after setup?

Ongoing support options can be discussed based on your system, users and service requirements.
